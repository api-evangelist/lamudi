# Lamudi Philippines

> The Philippines' leading real estate portal. Find houses, condominiums, apartments, commercial spaces and land for sale and rent across the country.

Lamudi Philippines allows searching for properties by location, property type and operation. Each search results page includes market statistics, an interactive map and nearby points of interest. It also features a home loans calculator and foreclosed properties.

## Property groups

- `house` — Houses: single-family houses
- `condo` — Condominiums: condos, studios, lofts, penthouses
- `apartment` — Apartments
- `commercial` — Commercial: offices, retail spaces, service offices, warehouses
- `land` — Land: residential lots, commercial lots, agricultural lots, beach lots

## Operations

- `buy` — For sale
- `rent` — For rent

## Listing search (SERP)

URL: `https://www.lamudi.com.ph/{operation}/{geo}/{type}/`

Multiple geographic levels can be combined: `/{operation}/{region}/{city}/{type}/`

Each SERP includes:
- Listings with price, floor area, bedrooms and bathrooms
- Market statistics: average price per sqm, price trends and area comparisons
- Interactive map with exact property locations
- Nearby points of interest: public transport, schools, hospitals

## Listing detail pages

Each listing has its own page with:
- Full details: price, floor area, lot area, bedrooms, bathrooms, parking and amenities
- Photo gallery
- Map location and nearby points of interest
- Agent or developer contact information
- Similar properties in the area

## Project detail pages

Each development project has its own page with:
- Project name, developer and location
- Available units: types, floor areas, prices and floor plans
- Amenities (pool, gym, security, etc.)
- Photo gallery and renders
- Contact form for the developer
